thesis:
The report describes a reproducible local pipeline for testing whether LLM plan quality changes when only the order of semantically equivalent PDDL actions is rearranged.

academic supervisor, practice reviewers, and classmates following the research presentation

from motivation and formal planning background to an experimental pipeline, evaluation methodology, software organization, and the logic of future statistical analysis
